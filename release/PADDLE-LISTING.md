## IMPORTANT: WHAT PADDLE IS
Paddle is Merchant of Record (like Lemon Squeezy) — they handle tax/VAT globally. They require business verification before selling.

## STEP 1: SIGN UP
1. Go to https://www.paddle.com/signup
2. Choose **Paddle Billing** (for digital products/SaaS)
3. Complete business profile: legal name, address, website (use your GitHub: https://github.com/SHIVAM73566/VentureX-ERP-CRM), support email

## STEP 2: VERIFICATION (Required Before First Sale)
Paddle reviews:
- Business identity documents (ID/passport works for individuals)
- Website/product page (your GitHub repo + Gumroad page both work)
- Support process (email support@venturexerp.com)
Verification takes 3-7 business days. You can build products while waiting.
