# FASTSPRING - LISTING & SETUP GUIDE

## STEP 1: SIGN UP
1. Go to https://fastspring.com/sign-up/
2. Choose **Contextual Commerce** plan (pay-as-you-go)
3. Complete seller profile: business info, payout method (wire/PayPal/Payoneer)

## STEP 2: VERIFICATION
FastSpring (Merchant of Record) verifies:
- Identity/government ID
- Product ownership proof -> link your GitHub repo
- Fulfillment readiness -> you deliver via instant download (OK)
Typical approval: 2-5 business days.
