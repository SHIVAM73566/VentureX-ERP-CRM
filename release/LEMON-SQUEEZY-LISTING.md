## Stop Renting Your Business Software. Own It.

Every month, businesses waste thousands on SaaS subscriptions they don't control. Prices increase. Features get removed. Data gets locked in.

Built on Laravel 13 with PHP 8.4, it combines **CRM, sales, inventory, procurement, finance, logistics, AI copilot, and support ticketing** into one unified platform — designed for agencies, trading companies, wholesalers, distributors, and growing businesses.

### What You Get

**8 Complete Business Modules**

- **CRM** — Manage customers, contacts, leads, opportunities, and your full sales pipeline
- **Sales** — Create quotations, sales orders, invoices, and payments with PDF generation
- **Inventory** — Track products, warehouses, stock movements, and SKU management
- **Procurement** — Handle purchase requisitions, purchase orders, RFQs, and supplier offers
- **Finance** — Full chart of accounts, journal entries, receivables, payables, and dashboards
- **Logistics** — Manage shipments, containers, and landed costs
- **Support** — Built-in help center, ticket system, error reporting, and documentation
- **Admin** — Users, roles, settings, import/export, audit logs, and system health monitoring
