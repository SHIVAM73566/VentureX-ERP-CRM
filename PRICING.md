## Card-free redemption (no credit card in the app)

Payment is handled entirely by AppSumo. **No credit card is entered in VentureX ERP & CRM** — activation is by code only.

1. Purchase the deal on **AppSumo** and open your order to reveal your **1 or 2 codes**. Keep codes private.
2. Install VentureX ERP & CRM and complete setup (see **`INSTALLATION.md`**).
3. Sign in as an **administrator** and open **Admin → Settings → Plan & Layers** (the in-app **Plan/Licensing** area).
4. Paste your **first code** into the redemption field and submit. This activates the **1-Code / $49** entitlement.
5. No card details are requested at any point — only the code.

## Stacking 2 codes ($98)

1. With the first code already applied, stay in **Admin → Settings → Plan & Layers**.
2. Paste the **second code** into the same redemption field and submit.
3. The plan upgrades to **2 Codes Stacked / $98**: full ERP/CRM multi-company, advanced reporting, role permissions, priority support, multiple license rights.
4. **Maximum 2 codes.** A third code cannot be stacked on this deal.
5. Confirm the active tier on the **Plan & Layers** screen. If a code fails, contact support with your **AppSumo order ID** — never post codes publicly.
