Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get started with VentureX?

After purchasing, follow the Installation Guide in the documentation folder. The setup wizard will guide you through database configuration, admin account creation, and initial company setup. The entire process takes about 10 minutes.

What server requirements are needed?

PHP 8.1+, MySQL 5.7+ or SQLite, Composer, Node.js 18+ for building assets. Works on shared hosting, VPS, Docker, or any PHP-compatible server.

Can I customize the AI providers?

Yes. VentureX supports NVIDIA Nemotron, Google Gemini, OpenAI GPT, Claude, and Ollama. You can switch providers anytime from the AI Settings panel. All data processing respects your configured privacy rules.

Is there a money-back guarantee?

Yes. We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee. If you're not satisfied, contact support@venturexerp.com for a full refund.
